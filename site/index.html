          Sigmastorm Path Finder

          Browse every area of Sigmastorm by level, see how they connect and what they hold (creatures, shops, buildings, quests, artifacts, portals), and find the shortest way from one area to another.

          How to use it

          • Use Route from / to in the header to get step-by-step directions. Stairs, sector maps, portals and the 25 energy teleport to Taulin Rad Lands are all considered.
          • Hover or tap an area for its info card. Drag areas to rearrange them; positions are kept in your browser and Reset layout puts them back.
          • Press / to search for an area.

          Where the data comes from

          The stairs, shops, artifacts, portals, buildings and their tiles are read from the tile map of every area in the Ultimate Sigmastorm 2 Guide. Missions and creatures come from the Sigma Storm 2 wiki, which is community-maintained and covers fewer areas, so some areas list no creatures or missions. Route lengths are estimates: walking is the straight-line distance and obstacles are ignored. Every portal can teleport you to any master realm, and you can teleport to Taulin Rad Lands from anywhere for 25 energy (counted as 25 moves); the route finder uses both.
